Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · applies to Easy Fuel Log 1.0 on Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux

In short

  • What you enter in Easy Fuel Log stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The only thing Easy Fuel Log can send anywhere is a crash report, and only if you turn crash reports on. They are off until you do.
  • A crash report says where the app's code failed. It never contains your entries, vehicles, notes or files, nor anything that says who you are.

Who is responsible

Easy Fuel Log is made by Denys Prykhodko, who is responsible for this policy and for the crash reports described below. Write to easyfuellog@gmail.com with any question about it.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter: vehicles, fill-ups and charges, expenses, service visits, tires, reminders, documents, the photos and PDFs you attach, and your settings. Easy Fuel Log keeps them in its own storage on your device and never sends them anywhere. We cannot see them.

  • Your system's backups. Android and iOS can include the app's data in the backups your device makes to your Google or Apple account, if you have backups turned on; on a computer, your own backups (such as Time Machine or File History) may include it. Those backups are between you and their provider, under its policy.
  • Files you make. A backup, a CSV or a JSON export goes only where you choose to save or share it.
  • Deleting. Entries you delete stay in the app's Trash for 30 days, then are removed. Uninstalling the app from a phone or tablet removes everything it keeps there. On a computer, uninstalling leaves your data folder, so that a reinstall finds it: delete it yourself to remove everything (Windows: %APPDATA%\Easy Fuel Log; macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Easy Fuel Log; Linux: ~/.local/share/Easy Fuel Log).

Permissions

Easy Fuel Log asks only when you use the feature that needs it, and uses each permission on your device only:

  • Camera and photos: to attach a photo of a receipt, a document or your vehicle.
  • Notifications: to remind you of service, documents and tire swaps.
  • Files: to import, export and back up, and to attach PDFs.
  • Network: only to send crash reports, if you turn them on.

Easy Fuel Log does not use your location, contacts, microphone or any advertising identifier.

Crash reports, if you turn them on

In More → About → Crash reports you can choose “Send” or “Don’t send”. The default is “Don’t send”, and you can change it at any time. While it is on, if Easy Fuel Log closes because of an error, it sends a report at once or, if it cannot, the next time it starts.

What a report contains

  • Where in the code it failed: the type of the error and the functions, in the app and the libraries it is built with, that led to it.
  • The app's version, and whether it is a beta.
  • Your device's make and model, its operating system and version, and technical details that help explain a crash: processor, memory and storage, screen size and orientation, whether it is an emulator or rooted or jailbroken, when the app was started, and on Android which permissions the app has.
  • On Android and iOS, a count of how often the app is opened and whether it closed normally, so the share of crashes can be seen. Each install of the app gets a random number for this count, made by the crash-reporting service; it is not linked to you, your device's identifiers or your entries, and it is gone when you uninstall the app.

What a report never contains

Your entries, vehicles, notes, photos or files; the text of the error, which could quote what you typed; your name, email or any account; your location; your device's name, time zone or language; and any record of what you did in the app before it closed. Easy Fuel Log removes these on your device, before the report is sent.

Where it goes

Reports go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a crash-reporting service, and are stored in its EU data region, in Germany. Sentry processes them for us only and does not sell or use them for anything else. Sentry is set not to store the IP address a report arrives from. Reports are deleted after Sentry's retention period, at most 90 days. We use them only to find and fix what made the app crash.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask what data about you we hold, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Apart from crash reports we hold nothing; crash reports are not linked to you, so we cannot pick out yours, but you can stop them at any time by choosing “Don’t send”. If you think we have handled your data wrongly, write to us, and you may also complain to your data protection authority.

App stores

Google Play and the App Store handle downloads, payments and any diagnostics you agree to share with Google or Apple, under their own privacy policies. We receive only the statistics they provide to developers, which do not identify you.

Children

Easy Fuel Log is a general-audience app and collects nothing from anyone, children included, beyond the optional crash reports above.

Changes

If a version of Easy Fuel Log ever stores or sends anything new, this policy changes before that version is released, with a new date at the top. Earlier versions of this policy are kept in this site's history.
