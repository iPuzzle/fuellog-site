Every fill-up, charge and repair, in one logbook

Easy Fuel Log records what your vehicles cost to run and tells you what it means: real consumption, cost per kilometre, what is due next. It works offline, needs no account, and keeps everything on your device.

What it does

  • Cars, motorcycles, trucks, vans, buses, boats, generators and tractors; bi-fuel LPG and petrol, electric and plug-in hybrids.
  • A fill-up or a charge in under ten seconds, with a keypad and a price that never overwrites what you typed.
  • Consumption from full tanks, cost per kilometre, monthly spending, price trends.
  • Expenses and service visits, tire sets and seasonal swaps, reminders by distance, time or engine hours, and document expiry.
  • Import from any CSV, Fuelio, Drivvo and Fuelly exports included (these three are being checked with real exports during the beta); export to CSV and JSON and full backups, always free.
  • English and Ukrainian, every common unit and currency, text up to 200 %.

Get Easy Fuel Log

Beta

Easy Fuel Log 1.0 is in a closed beta on Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux. To join, write to easyfuellog@gmail.com and say which devices you use and what you drive. Owners of LPG cars, electric cars and motorcycles are especially welcome.

Join the beta Desktop downloads

On the downloads page, take the .msi for Windows, the .dmg for macOS, or the .deb for Debian, Ubuntu and their relatives. The macOS build runs on Apple-silicon Macs. Windows warns once that the installer is unrecognised: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Support

Write to easyfuellog@gmail.com. Say which device and version you use (More → About → Version) and what happened. We never see your entries, so we cannot restore them for you: keep a backup (More → Data and backups).

Privacy

No account, no advertising, no tracking. The only thing Easy Fuel Log can send is a crash report, and only if you turn that on. Read the privacy policy.
