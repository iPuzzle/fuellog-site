What it does

Your car’s notebook

Fill-ups and charging, every expense, service visits, tires, documents and reminders, in one timeline.

01 · Fill-ups

A fill-up in under ten seconds

A keypad of its own, the price you paid last time one tap away, and the total worked out for you. A price never overwrites what you typed.

  • Price, quantity and total: type two, the third is worked out
  • Full tank, partial or a missed fill-up, kept apart
  • Warnings when a number looks odd, never a block

02 · Statistics

Real consumption, from full tanks

Worked out between full tanks, not read from the trip computer, and kept separately for each fuel, so a bi-fuel car shows LPG and petrol on their own.

  • Consumption, cost per kilometre and monthly spending
  • Price trends and each fuel’s share of the distance
  • L/100 km, km/L, MPG, kWh/100 km or mi/kWh

03 · Reminders

Service and papers on time

Reminders by distance, by date or by engine hours, whichever comes first, with a good guess at when you will get there at your usual pace.

  • Presets for oil, filters, brake fluid, the timing belt and more
  • Mark one done and a service visit opens, already filled in
  • Insurance and inspection dates, warned 30, 7 and 1 day ahead

04 · Vehicles

Any vehicle, any energy

Cars, motorcycles, trucks, vans, buses, boats, generators and tractors, measured in kilometres, miles or engine hours.

  • Petrol and diesel
  • LPG and CNG
  • Electric and plug-in hybrids

Tablets

Room for the details

On a tablet, the timeline and the entry you opened sit side by side, with keyboard shortcuts for adding one.

iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux come later.

And the rest

Everything a driver keeps track of

  • Expenses and service visits

    Repairs, parts, washing, parking, tolls and fines, and service visits with every part, its price and its warranty.

  • Tire sets

    Summer, winter and all-season sets: the distance each has done, tread depth, age and the seasonal swap.

  • Documents

    Insurance, green card, registration, inspection and warranty, with a warning before each expires.

  • Import

    From any CSV, Fuelio, Drivvo and Fuelly exports included (these three are being checked with real exports).

  • Export and backups

    CSV, JSON and full backups, always free. Your records never depend on us.

  • Your units, your language

    English and Ukrainian, every common unit and currency, dark and light themes, text up to 200 %.
